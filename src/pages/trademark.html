Trademark policy

The Ezeh Start code is free to use under the MIT License. The name isn't covered by that licence. This policy explains when you can use the name without asking, and when you need permission.

Short version. Build anything you like with the kit, and say it's "built with Ezeh Start". Don't name your product, company, domain or service after it, and don't suggest the project endorses you unless it does.

Why this exists

A name is how people know what they're getting. If a fork, a paid course, or an unrelated product could call itself "Ezeh Start", people would reasonably assume it came from, or is vouched for by, this project. The policy protects that trust, and it's deliberately generous about everything else.

Any name that begins with "Ezeh" and is used for software, a starter kit or a developer service is covered by this policy and needs permission.

Uses that need no permission

You may use the marks without asking, provided that all of the following are true:

  1. your use follows this policy;
  2. it's non-commercial, or it's one of the commercial uses listed as allowed below;
  3. it refers to the actual Ezeh Start software, unmodified or clearly described as modified;
  4. it doesn't suggest that the owner approves, sponsors or is affiliated with you, your product or your service when that isn't true.

Allowed, including commercially

  • Saying what you built on. "Built with Ezeh Start", "Based on the Ezeh Start starter kit", or "Powered by Ezeh Start" in a README, an about page, a footer or a case study.
  • Selling what you built. An application built from the kit can be sold, hosted or licensed under your own name. The MIT License already gives you that. This policy only asks that the product isn't named after the kit.
  • Writing about it. Articles, reviews, tutorials, talks, videos and comparisons that refer to Ezeh Start by name, accurately.
  • Describing compatibility. A package or add-on named descriptively, for example "Stripe billing for Ezeh Start" or "Invoices module for Ezeh Start".

Naming your add-on

Fine

  • "Stripe billing for Ezeh Start"
  • "Acme CRM, built with Ezeh Start"
  • "Teams module (compatible with Ezeh Start)"

Needs permission

  • "Ezeh Start Billing"
  • "EzehStart Pro", "EzehCRM"
  • "The official Ezeh Start teams module"

The names on the right read as if they came from, or were endorsed by, the project. The names on the left describe a relationship without claiming one.

Uses that need permission

You need a written trademark licence from the owner for any of the following:

  • Product names. Using a mark as, or as part of, the name of a product, application, fork, distribution, theme, plugin or company.
  • Commercial software built on or including Ezeh Start that's marketed under the Ezeh Start name, for example a paid "Ezeh Start Pro" or a resold copy of the kit carrying its branding.
  • Commercial services under the name. Training, courses, certification, conferences, consulting, hosting, support or design services offered as Ezeh Start services, for example "Ezeh Start Academy" or "Ezeh Start Hosting".
  • Domain names, handles and URLs containing a mark, such as ezehstart-pro.com, @ezehstartdev, or an app-store listing.
  • The logo, anywhere other than an unmodified reference to this project.
  • Merchandise bearing a mark.
  • Any service related to one of the uses above.

Forks and modified versions

You're free to fork the code, which is what the MIT License is for. If you publish a fork or a modified distribution:

  • Give it a different name. Remove the Ezeh Start name and logo from its branding, package name (composer.json name), default APP_NAME, and public pages.
  • You may say it's "a fork of Ezeh Start" or "based on Ezeh Start", as long as the fork's own name is clearly different and more prominent.
  • Keep the MIT copyright notice. Removing the name from your branding doesn't mean removing the licence.

How to use the marks

  • Write the name as Ezeh Start: two words, both capitalised. Use ezehstart only for code identifiers and package names.
  • Don't change the logo's proportions, colours or lettering, and don't combine it with other marks.
  • Where it's practical, include the notice: "Ezeh Start is a trademark of Kingsley Uchenna Ezeh."
  • Don't use the marks in a way that's misleading, disparaging, or linked to unlawful activity.

Asking for permission

If your use isn't listed here, or you'd like a licence for one of the restricted uses, email kingsley.uchenna.ezeh@gmail.com and describe what you have in mind. Permission is usually given where the use is accurate and good for the community. It may come with a short agreement to keep a stated quality standard, and it can be withdrawn if that standard isn't kept.
