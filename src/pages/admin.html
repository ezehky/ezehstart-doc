The admin workspace

Everything under /app-splash, grouped the way the sidebar groups it.

Live metrics with sparklines (TrendService) and a "waiting on you" queue. The arrangement is saved per account.

Content

Posts, categories, tags, announcements, FAQs (Markdown answers shown on the landing page), and policies.

Marketing

Campaigns, the campaign builder, templates, saved sections, sent history, and settings. See Email and newsletter.

Money

The transaction ledger, its review queue, manual adjustments, refunds and "check with provider". Payments (gate payments) shows webhooks, traces and which vendors are configured. Subscriptions (gate subscriptions) manages plans, prices, features and subscribers. See Payments and subscriptions.

Site configuration

Info, Security, Preferences, social handles, contact locations, currencies, email senders, notification types, a log viewer that reads the tail of the application log, and a raw JSON editor.

Shared with members

Profile, image library, video library. Members also have their transactions (with Add funds) and their subscription.
