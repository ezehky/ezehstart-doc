Third-party software

The kit depends on other open-source packages (Laravel, Livewire, Flux UI, Tailwind CSS, Socialite, spatie packages, dompdf, openspout and others). Each keeps its own licence, listed in composer.lock and package-lock.json. Flux UI is used in its free tier only. If you add Flux Pro components, you need your own Flux licence.

Code licence vs. trademark

The MIT License covers copyright in the code. It says nothing about the name "Ezeh Start" or its logo. Being free to fork and ship the code doesn't make you free to call your product Ezeh Start. The trademark policy explains what you can and can't do with the name.
