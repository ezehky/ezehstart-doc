Going to production

A checklist for turning the kit into a live product.

Identity and branding

  • Rename your product. Set APP_NAME and the site name under Site configuration → Info. Your product mustn't be called "Ezeh Start". See the trademark policy.
  • Replace the logo, favicon and support contacts.
  • Change or remove the seeded admin admin@example.test / password.

Legal

  • Replace the demo policies with terms, privacy and cookie policies written for your business. Publish them as a new version.
  • If you add analytics or advertising cookies, turn off the cookie notice and put a real consent choice in its place.
  • Decide on account-deletion and activity-log retention windows, and document them in your privacy policy.
  • Keep the MIT copyright notice that ships with the kit (see License).

Infrastructure

  • APP_ENV=production, APP_DEBUG=false, a real mail driver, and a production database.
  • A cron entry for the scheduler and a supervised queue worker.
  • Back up the site configuration file on the local disk as well as the database. It isn't a table.
  • Install jpegoptim, optipng and pngquant if you want uploads optimised.
  • Set the Turnstile keys before turning the captcha on.

Security review

  • Review every custom role's gate map. New screens start open for broad roles.
  • Decide whether two-factor, passkeys and social sign-in should be on.
  • Check who holds the impersonation ability.
