Email and newsletter

A template builder, campaigns, a newsletter with no extra table, and system emails an admin can restyle.

Template builder

Admin → Marketing → Templates builds emails from blocks: heading, paragraph, button, image, divider, spacer, columns, raw HTML, logo, favicon, social links, and blocks that pull in dynamic or related content. Design settings (colours, fonts, layout) apply to the whole template.

Saved sections (a header, a footer, a call to action, a promotional block) are designed once and dropped into any template or campaign.

Subjects, preview text and block content accept {{token}} and {{token | default: "fallback"}}. Tokens resolve the same way wherever they're typed.

Campaigns

  • Audience: specific addresses, all users, or everyone subscribed to chosen notification types.
  • Test sends, optionally previewed as a particular account.
  • Scheduling in the sender's own time zone, with recurrence: daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly or quarterly. Each occurrence becomes its own campaign.
  • email:send-campaigns runs every minute and sends in chunks of 200 recipients.
  • Sent keeps a history of what went out, and to whom.
  • Senders: the default address, or a custom domain.

System emails

A builder template can stand in for a built-in mailable. Assign it to a slot in SystemEmailEnum (currently login and welcome) through email_templates.system_email. The mailable uses the WithSystemTemplate trait. If no template is assigned, the Blade view is sent as before.

Newsletter

There's no subscribers table. An address is a users row: the account if one exists, or a new NEWSLETTER_SUBSCRIBER row if not. The subscription itself is the ANNOUNCEMENTS switch in notification_preferences. Someone who signs up in March and registers in June keeps the same row and subscription.

A subscriber row isn't an account: it has no password and it's excluded from listings and metrics. Templates insert an unsubscribe link with the {{unsubscribe_url}} token, which leads to /unsubscribe/{email}.

Announcements

The public popup shows the latest live announcement (a picture or an icon, a link, and an optional newsletter form) in a stacked or split layout. Older announcements aren't queued behind it. If none is live, the popup falls back to the plain newsletter sign-up.

Cookie notice

preferences.accept-cookies shows a notice on every page. It's a notice, not a consent gate: the kit sets only session and CSRF cookies. If you add analytics, turn it off and put a real consent choice in its place.
