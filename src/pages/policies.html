Legal pages and consent

Terms, privacy and cookie pages that your install serves to its own users, with consent recorded against the exact version accepted.

This page is about the legal pages your application serves. For the legal terms of Ezeh Start itself, see License, Trademark policy and Disclaimer.

The pages

/terms, /privacy and /cookies are written in Markdown from Admin → Policies and served as plain, crawlable pages with a contents sidebar. Each one is a case in PolicyTypeEnum, and adding a case gives you a new public page automatically. The public footer links them straight from the enum.

App\Models\Policy is a legal document, not a Laravel authorization policy. The kit uses no authorization policies at all.

Versioning

  • Published text is never edited. To change it, draft a new version and publish that.
  • Older versions stay readable at ?version=, because consent records point at them.

Consent

  • Terms and privacy are marked as requiring consent, so registration asks for it.
  • user_consents records the version accepted, with the timestamp, IP address and user agent.
  • When a replacement is published, every account has to accept it again.

Before you go live

The DemoSeeder policies only show the shape of a policy. They aren't legal advice and aren't written for your jurisdiction or business. Have your own terms and privacy policy written, then publish them as a new version.
