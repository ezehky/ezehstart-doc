Scheduler and queue

Several features only work on a live install if the scheduler and a queue worker are running.

Scheduled commands

CommandWhenWhat it does
activity:prune-logsDaily 07:45Deletes activity-log rows older than the retention window. Does nothing while the window is 0
account:send-deletion-remindersDaily 08:00Warns accounts five days and one day before deletion. Each send is recorded so a retry can't send it twice
account:process-deletionsDaily 08:15Anonymizes or removes accounts whose grace period has ended
blog:publish-scheduledEvery minutePublishes posts that have reached their time and notifies subscribers. Each post is claimed first, so overlapping runs can't announce it twice
email:send-campaignsEvery minuteSends due campaigns in chunks of 200 recipients and schedules the next occurrence of recurring ones
account:prune-unverifiedDaily 08:30Warns unverified accounts, then removes them after the grace period. Does nothing while verification is off
account:send-inactivity-remindersDaily 10:00Sends one "we missed you" per absence
payments:reconcileEvery 10 minutesAsks the vendor about checkouts still processing, and settles what has finished
payments:sync-subscriptionsHourlyKeeps subscriptions in step with their vendor, and ends expired ones and finished trials
payments:prune-tracesDaily 07:50Removes payment traces past their retention window

The account, blog, email and activity-log commands, and payments:reconcile, accept --dry-run, which reports what they would do without doing it.

Running the scheduler

In development:

php artisan schedule:work

In production, one cron entry:

* * * * * cd /path/to/app && php artisan schedule:run >> /dev/null 2>&1

The queue

composer dev starts a queue worker for you. In production, keep php artisan queue:work running under a process supervisor. Mailables and the in-app notification implement ShouldQueue, so without a worker no email goes out. Payment webhooks are processed on the queue too, by ProcessPaymentWebhook. Without a worker, a payment is only confirmed when the reconcile sweep reaches it.
